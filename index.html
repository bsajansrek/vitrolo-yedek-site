What we promise

  • We never ask for your passwords. Not for your email, your social accounts or your bank.
  • Draft first, pay after. You see your site before you pay anything.
  • A printed QR code never changes. Change a price later and the same code shows the new menu.
  • Your site stays live even without care. Care is optional. A site you stop caring for does not switch off.

From year 2, the only extra cost is your domain renewal: included with care, or paid each year at the registrar's price without it.

Questions people ask us first

Is the draft really free?

Yes. We build it, you look at it, and you only pay if you decide to go live. No card, no contract to see it.

Are there monthly fees?

No. Care is optional and paid up front for 3, 6 or 12 months. If you don't want it, you don't pay for it, and your site stays live.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is in your name from day one, and the files are yours once the site is paid.

You're in Türkiye. How does that work?

Very simply. Websites are made on computers, and we talk by email, phone or video call. Our design team is in Türkiye and we have a contact in the U.S. Our studio details are at the bottom of every page, and our company name and address are on every agreement and invoice.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

No one can honestly promise that. We build a fast, clear site and make it easy for people to call you. Search engines decide the rest.
