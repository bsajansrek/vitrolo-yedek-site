A small studio for small businesses.

Local businesses are great at what they do and short on time. Most web companies ask for money up front and leave you with something you don't control. We do it the other way around: we build first, you decide, and everything we make is yours.

Why “Vitrolo”

The name comes from the shop window, the vitrine: the first thing people see before they walk in. Our mark is a ring in three parts, one for your site, one for the map and directions on it, one for your menu, with a dot that keeps going around. That dot is the care that keeps it all up to date.

Who we are

Vitrolo was started by a video editor and motion designer who spent years making local businesses look good on screen. That's why our sites move well and feel finished.

Our design team works remotely from Türkiye. Our contact in the U.S. is the friendly voice you'll probably hear first.

We work while you sleep (sometimes)

Türkiye is 7 to 8 hours ahead of the U.S. East Coast. Send a change after you close up, and it's often done by the time you open. Our agreement lists the response times we actually promise.
