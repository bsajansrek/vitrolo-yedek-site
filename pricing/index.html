    Ask us about it

    How paying works

    • You pay after you've seen and approved your site. Not before.
    • One payment, in full. Then we register your domain in your name, and your site is live within 24 hours.
    • Secure card payment. You get an invoice in your name for every payment.
    • One click for your domain. The company that registers your domain emails you one confirmation link. Click it once. It's required for every domain.
    • From year 2, your domain renewal is included while you have a care plan. Without care you pay the registrar's yearly renewal price, and we email you 60, 30 and 7 days before it's due. If it isn't renewed, the site goes offline when the domain expires.

    Questions

    Why is everything paid up front?

    Because you've already seen the site. You're paying for something finished, not a promise.

    Is the domain really free?

    The first year is included with every site, registered in your name. From year 2 it is included with care; without care you pay the registrar's yearly renewal price.

    Do you offer discounts?

    One, and it is the same for everyone: the more items you pick, the bigger the discount. 2 items 12%, 3–4 items 17%, 5 or more items 20%. Care plans are not discounted.

    Can I add something later?

    Yes. Add a QR menu, a logo or a care plan any time. Ask us and we'll quote it from the prices on this page.

    Why can't I pick the Website and the Large site together?

    The Large site already includes everything the Website has, so you pick one or the other.

    Is there a contract?

    Yes, a short agreement in plain English. You read it before you pay.
