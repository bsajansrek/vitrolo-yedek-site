Questions

Do my customers need an app?

No. Almost every phone camera reads QR codes on its own.

What happens to the code if I stop working with you?

It keeps working. It points to your own domain, not ours.

Can I have more than one menu?

Yes, for example lunch, dinner and drinks.

Can I still use printed menus?

Of course. The QR menu is an extra way in, not a replacement.
