The brief

A local detailer whose customers are busy, often on their phone, and tired of calling just to ask about price. The site had to answer three questions fast: How much? When can you take me? Can I trust you?

What we built

  1. 1

    Five pages, each with one job

    Home, Services & Pricing, Book, Results, Contact.

  2. 2

    Booking request with a live estimate

    Pick a service, vehicle size and time, and the estimated price updates as you go.

  3. 3

    Prices by vehicle size

    Sedan, SUV or truck, one tap.

  4. 4

    Before-and-after slider

    Drag to compare.

  5. 5

    A call and book bar on phones

    Always one tap away.

  6. 6

    A quote form with photo upload

    For jobs that need a look first.

Screens from the Tidemark concept site. Images are stock samples.

Motion with a purpose

Every movement on Tidemark points somewhere: a light sweep walks you through the four steps of a detail, service cards stack as you scroll, and the before-and-after slider nudges once so you know it moves. Then it gets out of the way.

People who prefer less motion get a calm, still version automatically.

On a real site, it's all you

Your photos from your phone. Your prices. Your hours. A link where your real customers can leave a review. We never use stock photos on a live site as if they were your work.
